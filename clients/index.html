by Chaleur Atelier

the pâtisserie behind beku°

beku° is the frozen wholesale line of Chaleur Atelier Pâtisserie, a modern French dessert atelier in Larkin, Johor Bahru. Chaleur means warmth and atelier means workshop: we started as a small two-person pastry workshop, and today our team makes petit gâteaux, whole cakes and viennoiseries for our own shop and for cafés and restaurants across JB.

Every dessert is made by our own pastry chefs, with no pork, no lard and no alcohol. The same recipes go into beku°, frozen at their best for your counter.

Visit our shop: Lobby, Zennith Suites, Jalan Kebun Teh 2, Larkin · open daily 11am–7pm
