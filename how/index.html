how to thaw

For our frozen petit gâteaux. Thaw in the chiller only, never at room temperature.

Carton of 12 pcs

  1. Take out only the pieces you need today.
  2. Thaw 3–4 hours in the chiller (0–5°C).
  3. Once thawed, keep chilled and serve within 3–4 days.

Individually packed · coming soon

  1. Thaw in its own pack, no need to unwrap.
  2. Thaw 3–4 hours in the chiller (0–5°C).
  3. Once thawed, keep chilled and serve within 4–5 days.
  • Do not refreeze after thawing.
  • Serving idea: serve straight from frozen, like an ice-cream dessert.
  • Our bakes (Lemon Pound, Sea-Salt Brownie) are handled differently. Ask us.
