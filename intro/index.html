about us

café-quality desserts, ready when you are.

beku° is the frozen wholesale line of Chaleur Atelier F&B Sdn. Bhd., the team behind Chaleur Atelier Pâtisserie in Larkin, Johor Bahru. We make modern French petit gâteaux and bakes in our own kitchen, freeze them at their best and deliver them across JB.

For a café, restaurant or hotel that means a full dessert counter without a pastry kitchen: keep cartons in your freezer, take out what you need, thaw and serve. Every piece looks and tastes the same as the last.
