the pâtisserie behind beku°

beku° is the frozen wholesale line of Chaleur Atelier Pâtisserie, a modern French dessert atelier in Larkin, Johor Bahru. We started as a small two-person pastry workshop; today our team makes petit gâteaux, whole cakes and viennoiseries for our own shop and for cafés and restaurants across JB.

Same skill, different direction. beku° desserts are not our shop cakes frozen. Each one is designed with careful thought for the way a business works: freezable by design, a longer shelf life, easy storage, and easy for your staff to plate and serve. We start from your problem (waste, staff time, an empty counter) and build the dessert around it. And we never stand still: our chefs keep creating new, on-trend desserts and keep growing the range.

  • Longer shelf lifeeasy to store, less waste
  • Easy for your staffno pastry skills needed
  • Always something newnew, on-trend desserts, a growing range
  • No pork, lard or alcoholmade by our own pastry chefs
